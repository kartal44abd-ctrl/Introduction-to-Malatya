About Malatya

Located in the Eastern Anatolia region, Malatya is a city steeped in thousands of years of history. Home to the UNESCO World Heritage site Arslantepe Mound, it bridges ancient civilizations with modern culture, famously known worldwide as the capital of delicious apricots.

Must-Visit Places

Levent Valley & Glass Terrace

Featuring magnificent geological formations dating back millions of years and a thrilling glass observation deck overlooking the valley.

Arslantepe Mound

One of the oldest archaeological sites in Anatolia, bearing the traces of the world's earliest state formation and palace structure.

Günpınar Waterfall

A stunning natural wonder located in Darende district, offering crystal clear waters, lush greenery, and a peaceful atmosphere.

Famous Local Tastes

Kağıt Kebabı

A unique local specialty made of finely chopped lamb, tail fat, garlic, and peppers, slow-baked wrapped in butcher paper.

World-Famous Apricots

Malatya supplies a massive portion of the world's dried apricot demand, celebrated for their unmatched sweetness and quality.

Analı Kızlı Soup

A traditional, labor-intensive masterpiece featuring bulgur meatballs filled with minced meat, served in a rich tomato and chickpea broth.
